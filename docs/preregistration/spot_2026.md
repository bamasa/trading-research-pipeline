## Why this study

The pipeline trades perpetual futures only: Bybit's linear books and prints,
and Binance's futures bars. This study adds spot, where there is no funding,
no short without borrowed or held coins, a different fee schedule on every
pair, and, on Binance, no public order book.

It asks one question, in three forms fixed in advance: **does S25 pay on spot
at the fees charged on each day of a block whose spot books and prints nobody
has opened?** S25 is boosted
trees on a book's microstructure, a hold-first rule at 240 trades a day, a
50 s hold closed by time, taker in and taker out, one clip of 0.010 BTC.

- **The primary venue is Binance BTC/FDUSD.** The author worked on this pair in
  2025, so every 2025 day of it is inside the author's information set and
  none can be a blind test: the blind blocks are in 2026. Binance's public
  archives hold the pair's prints and bars and no book, so S25 itself cannot
  be computed there (31 of its 40 inputs are book features, a 32nd,
  `vwap_deviation_bp`, divides by the book's mid, and its label and its
  executor walk the book). The primary slot is S25's **prints-only variant**,
  S25-PF: S25's coordinates with its nine print inputs, on a grid of prints,
  filled by a side-correct print rule whose bias is bracketed and measured.
- **The secondary venue is Bybit spot BTCUSDT**, which has a 200-level book
  from 2025-04-29 and prints from 2022. There S25 runs as registered: refitted
  on spot books (S25-BB), and unchanged, the 2025 study's frozen fit applied
  to spot books with nothing refitted (S25-T).

Every verdict is taken at the fee the venue charged an account without volume
or holdings on that day, from a sourced, dated table; a day no sourced
interval covers is excluded. Cheaper tiers, and fees of zero, are measurements.

**The prior is negative** ([below](#expected-outcome-stated-in-advance)). The
study is built so that a negative answer says how far each slot was from
paying: every verdict carries its fee break-even.

## Expected outcome, stated in advance

**No slot is expected to pay at the verdict fee.** The arithmetic:

- A taker strategy pays its fee on the notional of each side, and turnover
  counts both sides, so its break-even fee per side equals its gross profit
  in basis points of turnover.
- On Binance BTC/FDUSD a Regular account pays **0 maker and 10 bp taker** on
  every 2026 day of the blocks; on Bybit spot BTCUSDT a non-VIP account pays
  **10 bp on both sides**. A taker round trip costs 20 bp on either venue, so
  a slot pays at the verdict fee only with a gross edge above 10 bp of
  turnover, nearly twice the 5.5 bp that S25 must clear on Bybit's perpetual.
- The cheapest taker fees with a dated source for the blocks' days are
  2.3 bp on BTC/FDUSD (VIP 9, since 2026-01-29) and 5.0 bp on Bybit (VIP 5).
  Bybit's Supreme VIP (4.5 bp) and institutional Pro 6 (1.5 bp) were read
  on 2026-10-22 to 23 with no earlier date. All are measurements.
- The prior rests on this repository's earlier directional studies on
  perpetuals ([`docs/findings.md`](../findings.md)).

Predictions, one per slot, fixed now:

- **S25-PF** killed by K1 on H.
- **S25-BB** killed by K1 on H.
- **S25-T** killed by K1 on H, or inconclusive with too few trades: its
  thresholds were set on perpetual probabilities and may trade at another
  rate on spot.
- Every slot's gross edge below 10 bp of turnover, so K-fee also fires.
- Not predicted: the sign of any gross edge at zero fees, and the size of
  the print rule's bias.

## Fees

**The rule.** Every fill pays the fee of the sourced interval of its venue
in force at the fill's time, in basis points of the fill's notional. Fees
are booked in the quote currency at the fill price. This is a convention:
the venues deduct a fee in the asset received (a buy's in BTC) unless a
discount token pays it, which is equal in value at the fill price; [the
short side](#the-short-side) checks the account both ways. A day is covered
when every instant of it lies in a sourced interval of its venue; intervals
may split a day, and on such a day, possible only in P, the book executor,
which charges one fee a day, runs at the day's higher fee of each tier.
**A day that is not covered is excluded on that venue**,
reason `fee not sourced`, like any exclusion; a unit test checks now that
every day of the reader-test days, W, D and H is covered at the verdict
tier. P's days are not covered yet: [the fee amendment](#amendment-protocol)
covers them by the procedure registered below, before P can open.

**How the code reads the table.** `spot/costs.py` reads it from the
configuration's `fees` block when it runs, so the fee amendment changes no
code. An interval runs from its start, included, to its end, excluded (UTC),
so an instant on a boundary belongs to the later interval; where intervals
overlap, each side takes the higher fee at every tier. Coverage is per tier:
only the verdict tier excludes a day, and a measurement at another tier on a
day that tier does not cover is refused for that day. The book executor's one
fee a day is the day's higher maker fee and its higher taker fee, each taken
apart. K-units compares every fill's fee with the table's at a relative
tolerance of 1e-12.

**The verdict tier** is the one an account holds without 30-day volume, coin
holdings or programme membership: Binance's **Regular** and Bybit's
**non-VIP**. No discount is applied: a discount needs the venue's token (BNB,
MNT) held in the account, a coin holding outside the verdict tier, and the
sources do not say whether BNB's discount applied to BTC/FDUSD's taker fee
at all. Every other tier is a measurement, labelled with what it requires,
and a fee of zero at a tier is labelled "conditional on <tier> eligibility".

|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Reader-test days | 2026-03-03, 04 and 05 | 3 | both | the spot readers' proof, stage 0's timing and the dry run; nothing else | freely |
| **W** warm-up | 2026-04-01 to 04-30 | 30 | both | the touch size of Bybit's spot book; the history of rolling statistics; never scored | freely, through the development access |
| **D** development | 2026-05-01 to 07-31 | 92 | both | the fits and thresholds of the walk-forward (test months June and July, 61 days) and the frozen ones; σ of daily results and the minimum detectable effects; the sequence-gap threshold; the print rule's calibration | freely, through the development access |
| **E** embargo | 2026-08-01 to 08-07 | 7 | — | a gap, so that H tests transfer rather than continuation | never fetched |
| **H** held out | 2026-08-08 to 10-21 | 75 | both | the three slots' statuses and every measurement | once, after the D amendment is committed |
| **P** prospective | 2026-11-01 to 12-31 | 61 | both | the confirmation of a candidate on H | once, only for a candidate, after H's results are committed, its last day is published on both venues and the fee amendment covers its days, by [its deadline](#status-rules) |

Why these blocks.

- **Every 2026 block starts after BTC/FDUSD's fall** in activity: W in April,
  when the pair is near the level it keeps from May, and D from May. The
  months from January to March hold the pair's 2025-like weeks and the fall
  itself; no block uses them.
- **One calendar for both venues**, so that the print rule's calibration
  compares the same days, and one access, one embargo and one read serve
  the family.
- **The reader-test days** lie in March, outside every block, so the spot
  readers are proven on days no block uses (no spot day of any year is on
  disk to test them on, and no 2025 day is read). They are not
  the 2025 study's reader-test days, 2026-01-05 to 07, which no successor may
  put in a block and which this study does not read.
- **D** gives the frozen fits 85 days of training and seven of thresholds,
  and a walk-forward over June and July gives each slot 61 out-of-sample
  days for its σ.
- **E** is a week no slot reads, so that fits frozen on 31 July are first
  scored a week later.
- **H** is every published day after E, to 2026-10-21, the last day the
  archives held when this was registered.
- **P** is the first two months after this registration, outside every
  information set. Its embargo, 2026-10-22 to 10-31, holds the registration
  day.

Days are independent: each starts flat, no position is held across midnight,
and the day is the cluster for every statistic. A day is the rows stamped
from its midnight to the next, as in the 2025 registration, §Blocks. Rolling
statistics may read earlier days of the same block, and of W before D. **H's
first day and P's start cold**, as does a day after a hole (a previous
calendar day without a usable plane on that venue): rolling state is rebuilt
from the block's own rows and the first 61 minutes from midnight are not
scored, 61 minutes being the longest lookback in seconds rounded up (a 60 s
window and 3,600 s of winsorising, as in 2025).

**Exclusions are per day and per venue**, counted by reason and reported per
block. On Bybit's book, the 2025 rules: more sequence gaps than
`max_sequence_gaps` (the 95th percentile of D's per-day gap counts, rounded
up, by the 2025 formula, recorded in the amendment), a `positive_prices`
error in `validate_book`'s report, a price off the day's tick grid, a
missing plane (an HTTP 404 only), an unreadable archive or an unfetchable day
under the 2025 fetch plan's terminal rules, and a day flagged `flatten_stale`
in any run of the block's read. On Binance's prints: a missing file (404
only), a day without a print, a price off the tick grid, an unreadable or
unfetchable file; a print slot's order with no later print of its side is
a counted measurement, never an exclusion ([fills](#fills)). On either
venue, also a day the fee table does not cover (`fee not sourced`). A day
excluded on a venue is excluded from every
series of that venue's slots. **A venue's block is unreadable** when more than
a quarter of its days are excluded on that venue; nothing is retuned and no
status changes on it ([status rules](#status-rules)). Each verdict on Bybit
is also reported with the gap-excluded days put back, as a measurement.

The reasons carry these names: `no_book_file`, `non_positive_price`,
`off_grid_price`, `sequence_gaps`, `no_prints_file`, `no_print`,
`missing_plane`, `unreadable`, `unfetchable`, `fee not sourced` and, on
Bybit, `flatten_stale`; a planned day a read never reached is counted as
`not read`, and a day not kept with no reason recorded as `not kept`. A print
archive with a header and no row is `no_print`. A day excluded on Bybit only
for `flatten_stale` has usable planes, so the next day is warm. Each plane is checked against
its own tick, derived from its own prices that day (`rpi` prints included);
Bybit's prints off their grid exclude the day on Bybit, as its book's would;
a day with a single distinct price has no tick of its own, which is noted and
excludes nothing. `max_sequence_gaps` is computed over every D day whose book
file replayed.

## Common settings

- **Instruments**: Bybit's spot BTCUSDT and Binance's spot BTCFDUSD, each
  keyed by venue and market (`bybit-spot:BTCUSDT`, `binance-spot:BTCFDUSD`),
  never by symbol alone, since Bybit's linear perpetual is also `BTCUSDT`.
  Tick and lot steps come from each venue's spot specification and are
  recorded in the amendment; the off-grid exclusion uses each day's own tick.
  The fields read are Bybit's `priceFilter.tickSize` and
  `lotSizeFilter.basePrecision` and Binance's `PRICE_FILTER.tickSize` and
  `LOT_SIZE.stepSize`; neither endpoint has been called yet.
- **The book grid** (Bybit) is the 2025 registration's: `data/bybit.reconstruct`
  at `grid_ms=100`, at most one row per 100 ms interval at its first update,
  stamped with that update's venue time, no forward fill.
- **The print grid** (S25-PF, and S25-PB on Bybit's prints): one row per
  100 ms interval that holds at least one print, stamped at its first print
  and carrying the state right after every print with that stamp; later
  prints of the interval belong to the next row; an interval without a print
  has no row. Prints are aligned backward, as in 2025: row `t` holds the
  prints in (s_{t−1}, s_t]. The intervals are [k × 100 ms, (k + 1) × 100 ms)
  counted from the epoch, so from every midnight, and every print counts,
  `rpi` prints included.
- **Timing**: decisions are made at row stamps; an order is live 10 ms after
  its decision; every quantity in seconds is measured on row stamps, and
  "`t + h`" is the first row stamped at or after `t + h`.
- **Clip**: 0.010 BTC a trade, every slot, no sizing; one position at a time.
  Results are also given per 100 units of quote of clip a day: the sum over
  the day's trades of net over entry notional × 100, the 2025 form without
  funding.
- **The day**: no entry decided before 00:01 or after 23:58 UTC; anything open
  at 23:59 is closed by a costed taker order; the accounting identity holds
  at every day's end, with turnover equal to the sum of fill notionals.
  There is no funding.

### The short side

S25 is two-sided, and spot has no short without coins to sell. **Registered:
shorts from held inventory.** Each day starts holding one clip of BTC and
quote worth three clips at the day's first print. A long buys a clip with
quote and sells it at its exit; a short sells the held clip and buys it back
at its exit. A round trip's P&L, fees and turnover are those of a long or a
short of one clip, so every slot keeps S25's trades unchanged.
**The inventory's own P&L**, the clip of BTC marked from the day's first print
to its last, is a separate line, reported and never inside `net_bp_trade`,
any kill or any status. **K-inv** voids a slot whose account would hold less
than zero of either coin at any event. It is checked twice: with fees booked
in quote, the [convention](#fees); and with each fee deducted in the asset
received (a buy's in BTC, a sell's in quote), the day then starting with two
clips of BTC, the second covering the fees of 1,000 buys at 10 bp. On the
balances below it can fire only through an accounting fault.

The inventory is marked at the day's first and last print in archive order,
of either side (on Bybit, its prints, for the book slots too). K-inv reads
the balances the positions move: the BTC a short sells and buys back, and
the quote a long spends at its entry and gets back at its exit, valued at
the entry's price; each round trip's fees and P&L are a separate ledger,
which K-acct reconciles. So it fires only when a position exceeds the held
inventory, never on the fees or losses of a heavy day (on the full
balances, the fees of about 1,000 round trips at 10 bp a side would exhaust
the quote; 20 s holds allow some 4,300 a day). K-inv fires below −1e-12 of a clip in BTC or −1e-9 of the day's
starting quote, so that rounding cannot fire it. K-acct is checked per booking: at the day's end the
BTC balance is the start's, less the fees deducted in BTC under the second
booking, and the quote's change is the trades' net, plus those fees' value at
their fills. A book exit's fill enters the account at the later of its
snapshot and its arrival.

Measured beside every slot, with no status: the **long-only** variant, which
drops every short decision before the one-position rule (so its trades
differ from the slot's), with K-drift's all-long version as its comparator.
Borrowing to short is not modelled ([choices](#choices-and-their-reasons)).

### Fills

**On a book** (S25-BB, S25-T, S25-PB by the book walk, and the calibration on
Bybit): the 2025 registration's event-time taker executor,
`backtest/taker.execute_day`, unchanged: the clip walks the first snapshot at
or after the decision + 10 ms across ten levels, an entry whose clip exceeds
the visible depth is skipped and counted, an exit's depth beyond the tenth
level is charged at the tenth level's price and counted, the late-snapshot
counters and `flatten_stale` (R16) as there; there is no funding. **Its fee
comes from the table only**: this study's `costs.py` builds the executor's
settings from the fee table, a run given the executor's default settings
(whose fee is the perpetual's 5.5 bp taker) is refused, and a test checks
both. **Ten levels**: stage 0 records, on the reader-test days, the share of
snapshots in which 0.010 BTC exceeds the ten visible levels on either side,
over the grid's rows (an interval whose first update leaves a side with
fewer than ten levels has no row, as in 2025, and is never traded nor
walked); if it is above 0.1%, the clip walks all 200 levels of the archive
while the features keep ten, and the amendment records it. The 200-level
replay keeps the ten-level grid's rows: `data/bybit.reconstruct` at 200
levels would drop an interval whose first update leaves a side with fewer
than 200, so the spot replay replays the book with the 2025 code's book
state and grid rule, emits a row wherever the ten-level grid has one, its
first ten levels equal to the ten-level replay's, and leaves a deeper level
the archive does not hold at that moment empty, which the walk reads as no
depth; such rows are counted. The day's checks (`validate_book`) read the
ten levels, and a deeper price present must be positive.

**On prints, the side-correct rule** (S25-PF, S25-PB, S25-PFT), new code:

- An order arrives at its decision + 10 ms. **A buy fills at the first
  buyer-initiated print stamped at or after its arrival**, a sell at the
  first seller-initiated one: a price at which a taker traded on that side
  at that moment.
- **Prints no order can reach.** Stage 0 checks each print archive for a
  flag of RPI fills or an equivalent. Flagged prints are never filled
  against (they stay in the features), and their share is recorded.
- The clip walks that print and the other prints of the same side with the
  same stamp, in their archive order, at their volume-weighted price. The
  part beyond that sweep is counted (`fill_beyond_sweep`) and charged at the
  sweep's worst price (its highest for a buy, its lowest for a sell); under
  pessimistic depth, at the worst price of a print of the same side stamped
  in the 60 s after the fill's print and before 23:59 for an entry, or before
  the day's end for an exit, or at the sweep's worst price if there is none.
  Stage 0 records the median sweep against the clip. No entry is skipped for
  depth or for wait.
- The wait from arrival to the fill's print is recorded and its distribution
  reported beside every print slot; a fill whose print comes more than 1 s
  after arrival is counted as `entry_late_print` or `exit_late_print`.
- **The exit is decided as the print label decides it**: at the first row
  stamped at or after the entry fill's print + `h`, or at 23:59 if that
  comes first. It fills by the same rule, never on a print before the
  entry's fill, and the next decision may be taken at a row stamped at or
  after the exit's fill print. This is a registered change from S25's
  executor, which decides the exit at the decision + `h`: on a book the two
  differ by about one 100 ms row, on prints by the entry's wait. Each print
  slot's realised hold, entry fill to exit fill, is reported beside it.
- **No later print.** An entry that no print of its side follows before
  23:59 is not made and is counted (`entries_no_print`), as the book
  executor counts `entries_no_snapshot`. An exit that no print of its side
  follows before the day ends is priced at the last print of its side before
  it and counted with that print's age (`exits_no_print`), the ages reported
  apart from the waits. Neither excludes
  a day: on a thin pair it marks a quiet market, not a data fault. No print
  of the next day is read.
- The label's fills and the executor's call the same function.

**The rule's details**, as `spot/fills.py` fixes them for the label, the
executor and the walk tables alike:

- Only reachable prints (not `rpi`) make a sweep, the pessimistic window and
  the stale price. The pessimistic window is (s, s + 60 s] after the fill's
  stamp s, the fill's own stamp excluded, and ends where the order may no
  longer fill: before 23:59 for an executor's entry, before the day's end for
  an exit; its price is the literal worst print there, which can be better
  than the sweep's worst, so the pessimistic bracket can be better than the
  rule, and worse of two is never better than either.
- A part beyond the sweep of at most 1e-9 of the clip is rounding: it is
  taken as none and not counted in `fill_beyond_sweep`.
- A fill's time, and the instant its fee is taken at, is its print's stamp.
  The executor's entry print must be stamped before 23:59, the label's before
  the day ends, an exit's before the next midnight, and an exit never fills
  on a print stamped before the entry's fill.
- Each decision's round trip is computed as if it were alone; the executor
  then applies the entry hours and the one-position rule. When no row is
  stamped at or after the entry fill's print + `h`, the executor's exit is
  decided at 23:59, and the label's round trip has no exit, so its row has
  no label (the label has no 23:59: a row after it serves the label's exit).
- An exit that no print of its side follows is priced, for the whole clip,
  at the last reachable print of its side stamped at or before its arrival,
  even one before the entry, or, when its side has no reachable print that
  day before it, at the last reachable print of either side
  (`exits_no_print_either_side`). It has no sweep, nothing beyond one, a
  negative wait (the print's age, reported apart from the waits as the
  fallback's age), and its fill time is its arrival. The next decision may
  be taken at a row stamped at or after the later of the exit's fill print
  and its arrival.
- The stale bracket's price is the last reachable print of the fill's side
  stamped at or before the order's decision, or the rule's price when the
  day has none before it. For an exit priced without a later print, the
  rule, optimistic and pessimistic prices are that print's, and stale reads
  the decision as for any fill.
- **Worse of two** is, per fill, the worst for the account of the rule's,
  the pessimistic and the stale price; for an exit priced without a later
  print, also of the last reachable print of the other side stamped at or
  before its arrival, so that a fallback to an old print of its own side,
  even one before the entry, is held to the price the other side last
  traded at. An exit priced at a print of the other side
  (`exits_no_print_either_side`) is held to that print: it pays no spread,
  and is counted.
- An order the rule does not fill (an entry no print follows, or an order
  not priced by the fallback) has no price under any bracket, the stale one
  included, so it enters no bracket's or calibration's summary.
- The optimistic bracket is, as named, the whole clip at the first print's
  price; with prints of one stamp out of price order it can be worse than
  the rule.

**The brackets**, each run on the true decisions and reported beside every
print-rule verdict: **optimistic**, the whole clip at the first print's price;
**pessimistic depth**, the part beyond the sweep at the worst price of the
next 60 s, as above; **stale**, the last print of the same side stamped at or
before the decision row; and **worse of two**, per fill the worst of the
rule's price, the pessimistic price and the stale price, as above. K-fill reads the
last one, less the thinned calibration's advantage
([K-fill](#kill-conditions-per-slot)). The brackets order four registered
rules; none of them is a bound on a fill a real account would get.

## Labels, features and fits

**The print label** (S25-PF, S25-PB, S25-PFT; new code). At row `t` with
hold `h`, for the clip: the entry fills by the print rule at `t` + 10 ms; the
label's exit order is decided at the first row stamped at or after the entry
fill's print + `h` and fills by the same rule. Long P&L = (the exit's sell
price − the entry's buy price) / the mid proxy at the entry fill × 1e4 −
2 × the label fee; short symmetric. Class `long` if the long P&L is positive,
`short` if the short P&L is, and, since prints can make both positive, the
larger of the two when both are, `hold` on a tie or otherwise. A row without
an entry or exit fill before its day ends has no label and is not a
training row. The label fee is 0 for the slot and 0 or 10 bp across its
neighbourhood; the label declares `h`, and the purge is derived from it.
A row has a label only when both its long and its short round trip are
complete, since the class compares the two. The mid proxy at the entry fill
is read from the day's prints at the fill's stamp; a row where it is not yet
defined (one side has not printed) has no label. Each label row records,
per hold, `reads_until_ns_<hold>`, the stamp of the last print that hold's
labels read, and `reads_until_ns`, the latest over the holds.

**The walked-book label** (S25-BB): the 2025 registration's, unchanged, on
the spot book, at 0 bp for the slot and 0 or 10 bp across its neighbourhood.

**The nine print members** of S25's list, rebuilt on the print grid:
`flow_imbalance_6`, `flow_imbalance_24`, `flow_imbalance_120`,
`trade_intensity_6`, `trade_intensity_24`, `trade_intensity_120`,
`flow_persistence_24`, `vwap_deviation_bp` and `trade_run_length_60s`.

- Their row windows (6, 24 and 120 rows) count rows of the print grid, so a
  window spans a varying time; this is a registered change of meaning, not a
  value chosen on data.
- `vwap_deviation_bp` divides a row's volume-weighted print price by the
  previous row's mid proxy. These are two registered changes of meaning:
  the mid proxy in place of the book's mid, and row `t − 1` in place of row
  `t` (the 2025 code divides by row `t`'s own mid).
- `trade_run_length_60s` is evaluated at print-grid stamps.
- Each is winsorised at the 0.1% and 99.9% quantiles of its own trailing
  3,600 s, as in 2025. None is in raw size units, so none is scaled.
- Every one, and every touch proxy of S25-PFT, passes
  `validation/leakage_timed.check_timed_causal`, with the prints cut at a
  time strictly inside a row interval, and `check_streaming_parity` before
  any D run (K-leak); `validation/leakage.py` checks only features built on
  the row registry. They are built in a
  registry of their own, never in the global one. The proxies are row state
  of the grid, so they are checked as features of their own, computed from
  the prints the check gives them (a day's state as their lookback), and
  the grid's proxy columns must equal those proxies at the row stamps; the
  members that read them are then checked through them. The checks run on
  a slice of the day from midnight that reaches past the longest declared
  lookback by two minutes, and the streaming positions lie past that
  lookback, so that every declared buffer drops history.
- `vwap_deviation_bp` is undefined (NaN) on a row without a previous row or
  without a mid proxy there.
- On a day that follows a usable day, the members' history is that day's
  last prints, as many as the longest window needs counted in rows as well
  as in time: on a thin day 120 print rows can span more than 62 minutes.
  The book slots carry 62 minutes, as registered.

**S25-PFT's touch proxies** are computed from every print, `rpi` included,
as the state right after each row's prints; a side's proxy is undefined
until that side has printed, and it carries across midnight from the
previous usable day. Its twelve members are the 2025 functions with the ask
and bid proxies as the level-0 prices and the sizes undefined, each with its
own lookback and 3,600 s of winsorising, declared on the trades plane of the
print members' registry.

**S25-BB's inputs** are the 40 features as the 2025 study computes them, on
the spot book, with `ofi_1` and `ofi_20` divided by the median touch size of

**Fits.** `gbm` (`models/gbm.py`), three classes, the repository's defaults,

## The development-period procedure (D only)

0. **Stage 0, on the reader-test days.** The spot readers are proven, each on
   a synthetic fixture and on the three days: Bybit's spot `ob200` archive
   replays as a block day replays (no `positive_prices` error, no off-grid
   price), with its message counts, median interval and sequence gaps
   recorded; Bybit's spot print columns, units and side strings are checked
   against the parser's contract; Binance's aggregated prints convert with
   the side flag mapped as the venue documents it. Per day, the fetch log
   records the book's message counts and median interval (the format probe
   saw 200 ms between messages, the documentation says 100 ms), the share of
   100 ms grid rows in which the clip exceeds ten levels, the share of `rpi`
   prints and the count of each side on Bybit, and Binance's time unit. If
   that share sets the walk to 200 levels, stage 1 replays Bybit's book at 200
   levels, and the features read the first ten. The fetch, replay, label,
   fits and scoring are timed there, and the print walk tables are checked
   against the print executor, entry by entry, on runs whose busy periods
   depend on the side. Stage 0's labels, fits and scores are discarded and
   never reported; only the timings, the readers' checks and the parity go
   to the amendment. A failure is fixed by a dated amendment before stage 1.
1. **Stage 1**: fetch, replay and features for W and D; no H or P file is
   on disk before its read. The study code is frozen before this fetch, by a
   dated note, `docs/spot_2026_code_freeze.md`, naming the commit on a line
   ``Code-freeze commit: `<sha>` ``. Stage 1, D and the amendment each start
   only if the study code (`src/`, `experiments/spot_2026.py`, the
   configuration, `pyproject.toml`, `uv.lock`) is unchanged since that
   commit, and D and the amendment only under the commit stage 1 ran under,
   which the amendment freezes in `compute.record`. A later line in the
   note counts only when a dated amendment names its commit (a correction),
   and it needs a new numbered D run, stage 1 included.
2. **Leakage**: every feature and every fold (K-leak).
3. **Fits**: the walk-forward and the frozen fits.
4. **Scoring**: every slot, measured row and neighbourhood cell on June and
   July, S25-T on the same days, the calibration of the print rule on Bybit.
5. **σ of each slot's daily series** over the 61 test days, and the minimum
   detectable effects ([metrics](#metrics)).
6. **A dry run** of the entry point end to end, July standing in for H; its
   outputs are discarded.
7. **The amendment** ([amendment protocol](#amendment-protocol)).

### Nothing is searched

No value of this registration is chosen on an outcome: the slots, their
inputs, holds, rates, label fees, gates and neighbourhoods are fixed here,
and D fits weights and θ, which read no test outcome. Every slot goes to H
whatever D shows. There is therefore no catalogue to hash, no choice and no
placebo search. Each slot's D series is shown against the 2025 registration's
two nulls applied to its fixed cell, as S25's were there: hour-block sign
flips (999 draws, seeds 0–998) and day re-pairing over every shift s with
7 ≤ s ≤ 54 of the 61 test days (at most n − 1 when a test day is excluded,
n the kept test days). These are descriptions; they gate nothing: they run on
each slot's daily `net_bp_trade`, its verdict series, with G and the two
walked P&Ls recorded beside each day, and a Null B run that flags
`flatten_stale` gives its day no value under that shift and is counted,
excluding no day.
A computation on a test day outside the entry point is logged with its date
and the days it read; since it can choose nothing, its only use would be to
motivate an amendment, which the [protocol](#amendment-protocol) forbids.

**The run log (the trial manifest).** Every computation on a D test day, each
numbered complete D run of the entry point and each hand run, appends a row
to the run log: its date, the run (a D run's number, or a hand run) and the
days it read. The log is kept in parquet parts under `data/spot_2026/`, never
committed. Its manifest, `experiments/results/spot_2026_trials.json`, lists
each part's sha256 and row count and the whole log's sha256 and rows; it is
committed with the D amendment, which freezes that sha256 and row count under
`compute.record.trials`. H's gate and P's refuse unless the manifest is
committed unchanged at the frozen values and the parts on disk are exactly
the manifest's.

## Metrics

- **`net_bp_trade`**, primary for every slot: for one round trip, its P&L in
  quote (a long's exit value less its entry value, a short's entry value less
  its exit value) over the entry notional, × 1e4, less both sides' fees in bp
  of the entry notional. A day's value is the day's net in quote summed over
  its round trips, over the sum of their entry notionals, × 1e4. The daily
  series has one value per day with at least one trade.
- **`gross_bp_trade`**, the same before fees: the result at zero fees,
  reported for every slot, labelled "conditional on a zero-fee venue".
- **`gross_bp_turnover`**: gross over turnover (both sides' notional), × 1e4,
  the fee break-even per side, reported against every tier of the venue.
- **IC**: per day, the rank correlation of `p_long − p_short` with the
  realised forward move over `h`, over all decision rows; on a book, mid to
  mid as in 2025; on prints, from the mid proxy at the entry fill to the mid
  proxy at the first row stamped at or after the fill + `h`. Its mean over
  days. On prints the entry fill is side-free: the first reachable print of
  either side stamped at or after `t` + 10 ms.
- **The daily series decides.** The mean, the day-level t, the Newey–West t
  (Bartlett, lag 5 positions), the median day, K1, K-days, the placebos,
  K-nbhd, σ_D and the minimum detectable effect are computed on the daily
  series, never on trade rows, through `evaluation/significance.assess` with
  one row per day; a series of fewer than two days fails the day count and
  enters Holm with p = 1. The one-sided p-value is
  `market_making/verdicts.day_t(series).p_one_sided`, Holm is
  `verdicts.holm`.
- **Minimum detectable effect**, recorded in the amendment per slot:
  `(3 + 0.84) × σ_D / √n_eff`, σ_D the standard deviation of the slot's daily
  series over D's 61 test days and `n_eff` = 75 × the share of those days
  with a trade; on P, P's usable days in place of 75, with σ_D and the share
  as frozen, so P's MDE is the frozen MDE × √(75 / P's usable days).
- **Beside every verdict**: trades a day, the share of days positive, the
  median day, the maximum drawdown, net in quote a day, the result at every
  tier, by side, the long-only variant, the inventory's own P&L, the slot's
  30-day traded volume, the counters (skipped entries, late snapshots or
  prints, `fill_beyond_sweep`, depth beyond the tenth level), and for a print
  slot every bracket.

## Status rules

**The family** is **{S25-PF, S25-BB, S25-T}, with m = 3** on H and on P.

**The bar.** A slot passes on a block when no kill condition fires and all
of these hold on its daily series: its one-sided day-level p-value passes
Holm's procedure at 5% with **m = 3**; the day-level t is at least 3; the
Newey–West t is at least 2; the median day is positive; and the series has
at least 20 days with a trade on H, 10 on P. At m = 3 Holm's first step
needs a t of 2.17 at 74 degrees of freedom, 2.29 at 19 and 2.51 at 9, so
the t ≥ 3 bar binds on every block. A slot enters Holm with p = 1 when it is
void, killed, inconclusive with too few trades, not assessed, or not taken to
the block.

**Precedence**, the same on every block, one status per result:

1. **void** — K-leak, K-units, K-acct, K-cap or K-inv fires; counted as
   killed.
2. **inconclusive (too few trades)** — K-trades: fewer than 100 round trips
   over the block. Nothing below is evaluated.
3. **killed** — any kill condition fires.
4. **inconclusive (fill beyond sweep)** or **inconclusive (capacity)** — the
   bar is met but a cap applies: for a print slot, more than 10% of its
   fills on the block have part of the clip beyond the sweep; for any slot,
   the median day's [capacity ratio](#measurements-without-a-hypothesis) is
   above 2%. Caps are applied after Holm and change no p-value. A cap
   applies strictly above its limit; the share of fills beyond the sweep is
   over every fill of the true run on the block, and the capacity median is
   over every usable day of the block, a day without a trade counting as 0.
   When both apply, the status is **inconclusive (fill beyond sweep)**.
5. **candidate** (on H) or **passes** (on P) — the bar is met and no cap
   applies.
6. **inconclusive** — otherwise; labelled "below the minimum detectable
   effect" when the mean is smaller in size than the MDE.

**P.** Only a candidate on H is taken to P. P is read once, in one session
for every candidate, after the last day of P is published on both venues and
a dated fee amendment covers P's days; every other slot and measured row is
computed in that session as a measurement.

**P's deadline.** The fee amendment is committed, and P's read is opened and
every process of it started, by the later of 2027-01-31 and 30 days after H's
results are committed, those 30 days counted from the committer date (UTC) of
the commit that first adds `experiments/results/spot_2026_H.json`. No P read
opens after it, and no process of a P read session starts after it; a
process started by then runs to its end. Until then a candidate's status is
"candidate (P not yet read)"; after it, unread, "candidate, not confirmed (P
not read by its deadline)", and the table below is final. Both are written by
the entry point's `status` and on the page while P is unread.

| On H | On P | Status |
|---|---|---|
| candidate | passes | **confirmed** |
| candidate | does not pass, no kill | **candidate, not confirmed** |
| candidate | a kill fires | **killed on P** |
| candidate | a void condition fires | **void** (counted as killed) |
| candidate | P unreadable | **candidate, not confirmed (P unreadable)** |
| candidate | P's read failed | **candidate, not confirmed (P read failed)** |
| candidate | not read by the deadline | **candidate, not confirmed (P not read by its deadline)** |
| any other status | not read | that status |

Under [the fallback](#the-information-set), "candidate" on H reads
"candidate (H inside S25's information set)", and every status in the last
column carries "(H inside S25's information set)". On H no other status
carries the mark before P; `void (defect found after the <block> read)`
never carries it.

**Unreadable and failed reads.** A venue's block with more than a quarter of
its days excluded is unreadable: on H, that venue's slots are `not assessed
(H unreadable on <venue>)`, the other venue's are assessed, and H is spent.
A read that fails is resumed at the same commit; if the same stage fails
again with the same exception, or the read has not finished after five
resumptions, the session closes, as in the 2025 registration, §Access: on
H, every slot is `not assessed (H read failed)`; on P, every candidate is
`candidate, not confirmed (P read failed)` and every measurement is not
assessed. The venue is named `Bybit` or `Binance` in these statuses, as in
`not assessed (H unreadable on Bybit)`; on P a measurement on an unreadable
venue is `not assessed (P unreadable on <venue>)`. A slot not taken to a
block reads `not taken to the block` and enters Holm with p = 1. The family is
assessed only when every slot has either its values or one of these preset
statuses; a slot with neither is refused, not passed over.

**No number** at a tier other than the verdict tier, at zero fees, under a
bracket or the long-only variant, with the gap-excluded days put back, of a
measured row, or in the inventory's P&L **may be described as the
strategy working.** Such numbers are measurements. In particular, no account
could trade either pair at zero taker fees on any day of W, D or H as far as
the sources show, so a positive gross edge is a statement about a venue that
did not exist for these pairs on those days.

### Kill conditions per slot

| Slot | Execution | Primary series | Kills | Reported beside |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S25-PF | taker, side-correct print rule | `net_bp_trade` at Regular | K1, K-label, K-placebo, K-drift, K-nbhd, K-days, K-fee, K-latency, **K-fill** | every bracket; both calibrations on Bybit (S25-PB); the waits, realised holds, late and missing prints; the share of fills beyond the sweep |
| S25-BB | taker, book walk | `net_bp_trade` at non-VIP | K1, K-label, K-placebo, K-drift, K-nbhd, K-days, K-fee, K-latency | the gap-excluded days put back |
| S25-T | taker, book walk, the 2025 frozen fit | `net_bp_trade` at non-VIP | as S25-BB | its trade rate against 240 a day |

Beside every slot: the long-only variant, the inventory's own P&L, the
result at every tier and at zero fees, its 30-day traded volume, its
capacity ratio, and the share of grid rows it trades.

Void conditions; the slot is recorded as `void`:

- **K-leak** — a feature fails its leakage check (`validation/leakage_timed`
  for the print members and touch proxies, `validation/leakage.py` for
  features on the row registry), or the fold-level leakage test fails; every
  result of the study is void.
- **K-units** — a cost (a fee, the walk of a book, a print fill) or the
  hold-first rule's input reaches the result through a normalised column
  rather than prices; or any fill's fee, in bp of its notional, differs from
  the fee [the rule](#fees) assigns it from the table for its venue, tier and
  time, checked on every fill of every run.
- **K-acct** — the accounting identity fails on any day, or turnover differs
  from the sum of fill notionals, in any run of the slot's read.
- **K-cap** — the traded position exceeds one clip either way at any event.
- **K-inv** — the account would hold less than zero of either coin at any
  event ([the short side](#the-short-side)).

Kill conditions:

- **K1** — the mean of the slot's daily series on the block is ≤ 0.
- **K-label** — the signal's IC on the block is ≤ 0.
- **K-placebo** — the true mean does not exceed each of: the 95th percentile
  of the 200 random-direction draws; the sign-flipped signal's mean; and the
  one-day-shifted signal's mean.
- **K-drift** — the true mean does not exceed the larger of the all-long and
  the all-short versions' means at the same entry times. As for K-nbhd's
  cells, a placebo or K-drift run without a trade on the block scores 0.
- **K-nbhd** — the median, over the slot's 12 neighbourhood cells, of each
  cell's mean on the block is ≤ 0; a cell without a trade scores 0. Only the
  frozen fits and θ run.
- **K-days** — half or fewer of the series' days are positive.
- **K-fee** — the fee break-even per side, the mean over the series' days of
  each day's `gross_bp_turnover`, is at or below the verdict tier's taker
  fee, 10 bp. It weights days equally, as K1 does, but divides by turnover
  rather than entry notional, so it can differ from K1 near zero; its main
  use is the report of how far each slot was from paying.
- **K-latency** — a positive result that is ≤ 0 when every order is live
  110 ms after its decision (on a book, one more 100 ms row; on prints, the
  arrival moved by 100 ms).
- **K-fill** (print slots) — a positive result whose daily series under the
  worse-of-two bracket ([fills](#fills)) has a mean ≤ 0 once every buy is
  also charged max(0, a_buy) and every sell max(0, a_sell), in bp of the
  fill's notional, as an extra fee: a_buy and a_sell are the thinned
  calibration's mean advantage of the worse-of-two price over the book walk
  on Bybit's days of the same block ([measurements](#measurements-without-a-hypothesis)).
  The charge is applied after the run, to the worse-of-two run's trades,
  whose fills it does not change, and the charged series is computed exactly
  from each day's sums: the day's value is (net − 1e-4 × (max(0, a_buy) × the
  buy notional + max(0, a_sell) × the sell notional)) / the entry notional ×
  1e4, all in quote. A negative advantage charges nothing. When a_buy or
  a_sell is undefined (no calibration fill on that side), K-fill fires on a
  positive result.

Not kills, but they set the status: **K-trades**, fewer than 100 round trips
over the block, makes the result inconclusive whatever its sign, before any
kill; **K-MDE**, a result that does not pass, with no kill, and is smaller
in size than the MDE, is "inconclusive, below the minimum detectable effect".
K-latency and K-fill fire only when the result is positive under the default
rules. The two caps, fills beyond the sweep and capacity, set the status as
the [precedence](#status-rules) says; they are not kills.

### Placebos

Each is run through the same execution, fee, account and day rules as the
true slot, on the same block and the true slot's entry decision times.
**On a book**, a taker's exit time does not depend on its side, so no
decision is skipped, as in 2025. **On prints it does**: a buy and a sell
fill at different prints, so the entry fill, the exit fill and the time the
position is busy until all change with the side. Each placebo of a print
slot or measured row, the all-long and all-short runs of K-drift, and the
hour flips of Null A on D therefore re-apply the print executor's
one-position rule to their own fills: a true decision that arrives while
the run's own position is open, before its exit fill's print, is skipped,
and the skips are counted and reported beside the run. K-cap holds in every
run.

| Placebo | What changes | Seeds |
|---|---|---|
| sign-flipped | every direction reversed | — |
| random direction | each direction drawn at random | 200 draws, seeds 0–199 |
| one-day shift | each usable day takes the previous usable day's signal tape at the same time of day, circularly over the block's usable days, stepping over excluded days as in 2025; a shift run that flags `flatten_stale` excludes its day like any run, and when that day is the block's first usable one the next usable day's shift is redone from the last | — |
| all-long, all-short | one direction at every entry time (K-drift) | — |

The book slots' placebos, neighbourhood cells and K-latency come from walk
tables built from the 2025 executor's walk. The print slots' walk tables,
built from the print rule's fill function, store for every decision row both
sides' entry fill print, exit fill print and busy-until time; stage 0 proves
them equal to the print executor entry by entry, on runs whose busy periods
depend on the side included. A print slot's neighbourhood cells and K-latency
re-run are full runs of the print executor's rule on the rows passing θ.
G and the two walked P&Ls are the 2025 ledger's: per trade, G = side ×
(the long P&L − the short P&L) / 2 at the trade's decision row, in bp. On
prints one side can have no fill at a row where the other has one; such a
trade has no G, and a day's G and its two parts are means over the trades
that have one. A day without a trade scores 0.

## Measurements without a hypothesis

- **The print rule's calibration (FILL-S).** On Bybit's D and H, the
  decisions of S25-PB and of S25-BB are priced both by the print rule
  (primary and every bracket) and by the book walk. Reported per side: the
  mean and median difference of the fill price in bp of mid, the share of
  late prints, and each slot's `net_bp_trade` both ways (S25-BB by the book
  walk, its true run, and by the print rule; S25-PB by the print rule, its
  true run, and by the book walk; each the mean over the block's kept Bybit
  days). It is a measurement: a bias measured on a busy venue need not hold
  on a thin one.
  Each order of the book-walk run is priced by the print rule at its own
  arrival: an entry only at a print before 23:59, an exit at any print before
  the day ends and by the fallback if none follows, neither held to a print
  after an entry fill (the book run's entry has none); the stale bracket
  reads the order's own decision. Differences are in bp of the mid of the
  snapshot the walk used.
- **The thinned calibration (FILL-S thinned)**, on Bybit's D, H and, if read,
  P days. Each day, Bybit's spot prints are thinned at random to BTC/FDUSD's
  count of aggregated prints on the same day (all are kept when Bybit has
  fewer; a day excluded on either venue is not used), with seeds 0–9.
  S25-PB's decisions, made on the full prints, are priced by the print rule
  and every bracket on the thinned prints, and by the book walk. Reported
  per side and seed: the mean and median advantage of each bracket's price
  over the book walk, in bp of mid, positive when the print rule is better
  for the account. a_buy and a_sell, the worse-of-two bracket's mean
  advantage per side over every fill of the ten seeds on the block, are what
  [K-fill](#kill-conditions-per-slot) reads. The thinning draws uniformly
  without replacement from all of Bybit's prints of the day, `rpi` included
  (`numpy.random.default_rng(seed).choice`), and keeps the archive's order.
  a_buy and a_sell pool every fill of every seed and day, so busier days
  weigh more; an order the print rule cannot price is left out.
- **Capacity.** Each day, the ratio of a slot's taker volume on each side to
  the venue's taker volume on that side from its prints, the larger of the
  two; its median over the block's days is reported beside every slot,
  S25-PB and the thinned calibration (on the thinned prints), and above 2%
  it caps a slot's status ([status rules](#status-rules)). Volumes are in
  BTC, every print counting (`rpi` prints included, though no order reaches
  them); a side on which the slot traded and the venue printed nothing gives
  an infinite ratio, which the day's record holds as missing and the read
  takes back as infinite on a day with a trade; a day without a trade gives 0.
  The thinned calibration's median is over the Bybit days it is computed on
  (Binance's day usable too), a day without a book-walk trade of S25-PB
  counting 0.
- **The measured rows** S25-PB and S25-PFT ([the slots](#the-slots)),
  each on its blocks with K1, K-label, K-placebo, K-drift, K-days, K-fee,
  K-latency and K-fill evaluated and reported; K-nbhd is not computed, since
  each is fitted for its own cell only. No precedence is applied and no
  status is given.
- **Activity beside the series**: each venue's prints a day, beside every
  daily series, so that a result can be read against how much traded.
- **The edge ladder**, one table, each step labelled with the one thing that
  changed: S25-T on spot books (no refit); S25-BB (refit on spot books);
  S25-PB by the book walk, then by the print rule (prints only, same venue);
  S25-PF (the other venue); each at zero fees, at the verdict tier and at
  the cheapest tier with a dated source for the block's days.

unfetchable day), each archive's checksum is checked before it is read
(Binance's published `.CHECKSUM` for its files), and archives are deleted
after their replay or conversion. Every spot file lives under
`data/spot/<venue>/<plane>/<symbol>/`. Every day a stage asks for is
requested, listed or not, and a 404 on a day the head check listed is noted.
Bybit's book is replayed by at most four workers. A Binance file is never
read unchecked: a `.CHECKSUM` that cannot be fetched (a 404 included) is a
transient failure, retried until the day is unfetchable; an archive whose
sha256 differs from it is corrupt, and the day is unreadable after a second
mismatch.

**Disk.** Replayed at ten levels, a perpetual BTCUSDT day of 2024 took 40 to
60 MB; stage 0 measures a spot day. At that rate W and D take 4.9 to 7.3 GB and
H 3.0 to 4.5 GB; prints in parquet, under 1 GB; the every-tenth-row feature
stores, about 2 GB; models, tapes and results, under 3 GB; archives in
flight, under 1 GB with four workers. **The peak is about 12 to 19 GB.** On
2026-10-22, 66 GiB were free, and 59 GiB on 2026-10-24. The 2025 study's
registered peak, 46 to 53 GB, is its peak on D, not what it keeps: it
deletes W's and D's features after its H read and keeps its books, prints,
models and results, which may serve a defect check after its last read, so
this study writes and deletes nothing of it; the order of the two studies
is left to the disk check below (as amended on 2026-10-25: before that, no
file of this study was to be fetched before the 2025 study's last read). Before each
stage, stage 0 included, the fetch measures free disk and starts only if it
exceeds the stage's budget by 10 GB, about 29 GB for stage 1; otherwise the
stage waits. Each measurement is recorded, and the amendment carries them.
Every stage takes the peak's upper end, 19 GB, as its budget, so each needs
more than 29 GB free; a waiting stage measures again every 600 s, and every
measurement is appended to a JSON record.

**Compute**, on the machine of the 2025 study: about 200 book days replayed
at 2 to 3 minutes each on at most 4 workers, 2 to 3 hours; 42 boosted fits at
1 to 2 minutes each; scoring, placebos and neighbourhoods from walk tables,
minutes per block; under a day of wall time for D in all, a few hours for
each later read. Stage 0's timing on the reader-test days replaces these
estimates in the amendment.

## Amendment protocol

**The information-set amendment**, if it is made, comes before stage 0 and
before any spot file is fetched: the owner's confirmation of three end
dates ([the information set](#the-information-set)). It sets
`information_set.confirmed` in the configuration, records the configuration's
new sha256, and changes nothing else. Without it, the fallback holds.

**The D amendment**, after D and before H is read, is one dated entry under
[Amendments](#amendments), written by the entry point and reviewed before it
is committed. It records: the days excluded on D, per venue and reason; the
sequence-gap threshold by its formula; each instrument's tick and lot steps;
the reader-test results (each day's message counts, median interval,
sequence gaps and findings, and the print parsers' checks); the sha256 of
every frozen fit, of each slot's frozen file and of the fitted-values file;
every θ of every neighbourhood cell; σ_D and the minimum detectable effect of
each slot; the D series' summaries against the two nulls, labelled
"development"; both calibrations on D; the compute record, with stage 0's
checks (the share of snapshots beyond ten levels and the walk depth it sets,
the share of unreachable prints, the median sweep against the clip) and the
free disk measured at each stage's start; and the
configuration's sha256 after the frozen values are written into its
placeholders, on a line of the form ``Frozen configuration sha256:
`<hash>` ``. Each value has one placeholder in the configuration, commented
"frozen by the D amendment". Nothing else may change. The frozen values take
these shapes: `reader_test_result` {instrument: {day: {status, stream,
findings, passes}}} on the three days; `excluded_days_D` {instrument: [[day,
reason]]}, each day of W or D;
`instrument_spec` {instrument: {tick, lot}}; the walk 10 or 200;
`thresholds` keyed by S25-PF, S25-BB, S25-PB and S25-PFT (S25-T's are the
2025 study's); `sigma_d` and the MDEs keyed by the family's slots;
`frozen_slot_files_sha256` keyed by the five rows; and `compute.record`
holding the run log's sha256 and row count under `trials` and the
code-freeze commit stage 1 ran under at `code_freeze.commit`.

**A correction before H** (a bug in a reader, a feature, the label, the
print rule, the executor or the accounting) is made only by a further dated
amendment that states it and, if it could change a D number, re-runs D in
full before anything is frozen; complete D runs are numbered. Every change to
the study code after the code-freeze note is such a correction. No amendment
may cite a D number, a result of the 2025 study or anything seen on spot data
as its reason, except a fault it names. An amendment that changes the
configuration outside its placeholders records the new null-placeholder
sha256 on a line of the form ``Null-placeholder sha256: `<hash>` `` under
[Amendments](#amendments), before the code freeze; the loader accepts the
latest such line, at the H and P gates only one the document held at the
code-freeze commit, and refuses a superseded hash.

**The fee amendment** is the only amendment after H: it transcribes, by the
procedure registered under [Fees](#fees), the schedules in force on P's days,
by [P's deadline](#status-rules) and before P is opened, and adds intervals
only: each on one line under `fees.intervals.<instrument>`, starting at or
after 2026-10-22 00:00 UTC, overlapping no other, with its sources and the
verdict tier's fees, added only to a configuration already frozen, so that
removing them gives H's sha256; the document's amendments are only appended
to.

**After H is read**, nothing is changed and nothing is re-run under another
rule. **A defect found after a read can only lower a status**: it is stated
in `docs/spot_2026_defects.md`, naming the mechanism by which it could have
raised a result; every status it could affect becomes `void (defect found
after the <block> read)`, which counts as killed and enters Holm with p = 1
on later blocks. Nothing can raise a status; a corrected run is a successor
registration.

**The ledger rule.** Only reads through the ledger count. A number computed
on H or P outside it is not a result and cannot change a status.

- **The ask and the bid at a Binance order's arrival.** There is no book;
  the print rule is a proxy, bracketed, and calibrated on another venue.
- **S25 itself on BTC/FDUSD.** S25-PF keeps S25's hold, rate, clip, model
  and nine inputs, but its grid has rows only at prints, so the hold-first
  rule at 240 a day chooses among far fewer rows than on S25's 100 ms book
  grid: it is a less selective strategy. The share of grid rows each slot
  trades is reported beside S25-BB's.
- **What S25 earned on BTC/FDUSD in 2025.** Those days are inside the
  author's information set, and no day of 2025 is read.
- **The pair as it traded before 2026-01-29.** 2026's BTC/FDUSD is a thinner
  market; a result on H is about the pair after the change.
- **The fee a given account paid.** Tiers depend on volume and holdings; the
  verdict is at the tier that needs neither, and whether a BNB discount
  applied to a Regular account's taker fee on the pair is not known.
- **Capacity.** The clip is 0.010 BTC and own impact is not modelled; on a
  thin BTC/FDUSD day a clip can be a large share of a minute's prints. Its
  effect is limited only by the two caps (10% of fills beyond the sweep, 2%
  of the venue's same-side taker volume on the median day).
- **A real fill on BTC/FDUSD.** The verdict is positive only under the least
  favourable of the four registered brackets, less the thinned
  calibration's advantage, which is not a bound on a real fill: after a
  sweep, or when quotes widen without a print, the touch can be worse than
  any print. A live record of Binance's BTCFDUSD depth for P would bound it;
  it is not registered ([choices](#choices-and-their-reasons)).
- **Shorting through margin.** Shorts come from held inventory; borrow costs,
  borrow limits and liquidation are not modelled.
- **Quote-currency risk.** Results are in FDUSD or USDT and never converted;
  a stablecoin leaving its peg moves the pair's prices and is in the result.
- **Passive entry on spot.** Without a book on Binance no queue can be
  simulated; the 2025 study measures passive entry on the perpetual.
- **Anything beyond two pairs, two venues and the 2026 blocks.**
- **When the ledger's entries were made, to anyone but the owner.** The owner
  keeps intermediate work unpublished: the ledger's open entries, the D
  amendment, the reads' results and every commit before the study ends are

  of when each was made. The order of the reads and the amendments rests on
  the local history alone, and the ledger checks an open entry against
  HEAD's history, not against a remote.

## Choices and their reasons

- **The primary venue is the pair the question names**, Binance BTC/FDUSD,
  though it has no book; Bybit's spot book is the secondary venue, where S25
  runs as registered and where the print rule can be checked against a book.
- **Every block in 2026 after the pair's fall in activity**: 2025 is inside
  the author's information set, and January to March 2026 hold the end of
  the 2025 market and the fall itself, so a block there would mix two
  markets. One calendar serves both venues.
- **No day of 2025 is read.** The 2025 registration spends its H, F and P
  for every configuration of its catalogue, S25 included, and confines any
  2026 confirmation of them to dates after 2025-12-31. Its text reads that
  rule by date, not by venue, so a descriptive block on BTC/FDUSD in the
  last quarter of 2025, considered in an earlier draft, is not registered.
- **P has a deadline**, so that after H nobody can choose whether a
  candidate is ever tested: a candidate whose P is not read in time ends as
  "candidate, not confirmed".
- **A prospective P.** H alone can make a slot no more than a candidate; P's
  days lie after this registration, outside every information set, and cost
  nothing unless a slot passes on H.
- **Shorts from held inventory, not long-only.** Inventory keeps S25's
  trades unchanged, so the test is of S25 and not of a one-sided strategy;
  long-only is measured beside it. Borrowing was not chosen: it needs a margin
  account and carries liquidation risk; its cost, 0.35% to 1.3% a year on
  Binance in the window (B20), under 0.02 bp an hour, would not move a
  verdict either way.
- **The nine print members as the slot, the twelve touch proxies measured.**
  The nine are S25's own members; the proxies add construction choices made
  of prints. The 19 size and depth members have no analogue.
- **The side-correct print rule.** It prices a fill at a price a taker paid
  on that side at that moment. The last print before the decision is stale
  and rewards predicting the past; one-second bars with an assumed spread
  would make the result mostly the assumed spread. No entry is skipped for
  its wait, because a skip conditioned on a print following soon selects
  decisions by what happens after them; the one exception, an entry that no
  print of its side follows before 23:59, mirrors the book executor's
  `entries_no_snapshot` and is counted. K-fill holds a verdict to the
  least favourable of the four registered brackets, less the advantage that
  bracket shows over a book on Bybit's prints thinned to BTC/FDUSD's count;
  neither is a bound on a real fill.
- **The thinned calibration binds and the full one does not.** On Bybit's
  BTCUSDT the next print of a side comes within milliseconds, so a bias that
  appears only on a thin pair cannot show there; thinning to BTC/FDUSD's
  daily count is the closest registered stand-in for the thin pair.
- **No live record of Binance's depth.** Recording Binance's public BTCFDUSD
  depth stream from 2026-11-01, sealed by hash and unread until P, would let
  a slot be confirmed on a book walk; it needs the owner's approval of an
  exception to the ledger, and is not registered.
- **Caps of 10% and 2%.** A print slot whose fills often run beyond the
  sweep, or whose trades are a sizable share of the pair's taker volume, is
  priced by a rule that assumes no impact; the caps make such a result
  inconclusive rather than a candidate.
- **The verdict tier is the one an account holds with no volume or
  holdings.** A slot trading 240 clips a day has volume of its own, which is
  reported; but tiers also need coin holdings, and 2026's thresholds were not
  transcribed, so no other tier is assumed.
- **S25-T beside S25-BB.** S25-T has no degree of freedom left; S25-BB asks
  whether the recipe works when allowed to learn spot's scales. S25-T's
  inputs keep its own scale, so that it sees what it was trained on.
- **No search, m = 3, neighbourhoods of 12.** This is a confirmation of S25,
  so nothing is chosen on an outcome and no placebo search is needed. The
  neighbourhood keeps S25's hold, rate and label-fee axes; its label-fee step
  is each venue's verdict taker fee, as 5.5 bp was on the perpetual; the
  regime axis is dropped, since S25 has no regime policy and the detector's
  flags are not computed on spot.
- **Pairs not taken.** Binance BTC/U charged nothing to anyone from
  2026-04-17 to 07-16 (B17), but that window would have to hold both a
  development and a held-out block in 91 days. Binance BTC/USDT and BTC/USDC
  and Bybit's BTCUSDC were not taken because the question names BTC/FDUSD;
  no fee of theirs is transcribed with a source here, and none is relied on.
- **The reader-test days in March**, outside every block and apart from the
  2025 study's.
- **Nothing is fetched before the 2025 study's last read**: the two studies'
  disk peaks do not fit together on the machine, and the 2025 study's data
  roots, environment and code stay untouched until its reads end.

**Data and the fetch**

- Planes `book` and `prints`, files at `data/spot/<venue>/<plane>/<symbol>/<day>.parquet`: one root per venue, plane and symbol, apart from the 2025 study's.

- Bybit's book replayed at the walk depth when stage 0 sets 200 levels: the walk needs its levels on disk.
- The format probe's 200 ms between messages is measured again at stage 0 and sets nothing: fourteen messages are not a day.
