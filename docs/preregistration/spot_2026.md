## Why this study

The pipeline trades perpetual futures only: Bybit's linear books and prints,
and Binance's futures bars. This study adds spot, where there is no funding,
no short without borrowed or held coins, a different fee schedule on every
pair, and, on Binance, no public order book.

It asks one question, in three forms fixed in advance: **does S25 pay on spot
at the fees charged on each day of a block nobody has seen?** S25 is boosted
trees on a book's microstructure, a hold-first rule at 240 trades a day, a
50 s hold closed by time, taker in and taker out, one clip of 0.010 BTC.

- **The primary venue is Binance BTC/FDUSD.** The author worked on this pair in
  2025, so every 2025 day of it is inside the author's information set and
  none can be a blind test: the blind blocks are in 2026. Binance's public
  archives hold the pair's prints and bars and no book, so S25 itself cannot
  be computed there (31 of its 40 inputs read the book, and its label and its
  executor walk it). The primary slot is S25's **prints-only variant**,
  S25-PF: S25's coordinates with its nine print inputs, on a grid of prints,
  filled by a side-correct print rule whose bias is bracketed and measured.
- **The secondary venue is Bybit spot BTCUSDT**, which has a 200-level book
  from 2025-04-29 and prints from 2022. There S25 runs as registered: refitted
  on spot books (S25-BB), and unchanged, the 2025 study's frozen fit applied
  to spot books with nothing refitted (S25-T).

Every verdict is taken at the fee the venue charged an account without volume
or holdings on that day, from a sourced, dated table; a day no sourced
interval covers is refused. Cheaper tiers, and fees of zero, are measurements.

**The prior is negative** ([below](#expected-outcome-stated-in-advance)). The
study is built so that a negative answer says how far each slot was from
paying: every verdict carries its fee break-even.

## Expected outcome, stated in advance

**No slot is expected to pay at the verdict fee.** The arithmetic:

- A taker strategy pays its fee on the notional of each side, and turnover
  counts both sides, so its break-even fee per side equals its gross profit
  in basis points of turnover.
- On Binance BTC/FDUSD a Regular account pays **0 maker and 10 bp taker** on
  every 2026 day of the blocks; on Bybit spot BTCUSDT a non-VIP account pays
  **10 bp on both sides**. A taker round trip costs 20 bp on either venue, so
  a slot pays at the verdict fee only with a gross edge above 10 bp of
  turnover, nearly twice the 5.5 bp that S25 must clear on Bybit's perpetual.
- The cheapest taker fee published in the window is 2.3 bp on BTC/FDUSD
  (VIP 9, since 2026-01-29), 4.5 bp for Bybit's best retail tier (Supreme
  VIP) and 1.5 bp for its institutional Pro 6. These are measurements.
- The repository's earlier directional studies on perpetuals did not clear
  far smaller fees.

Predictions, one per slot, fixed now:

- **S25-PF** killed by K1 on H.
- **S25-BB** killed by K1 on H.
- **S25-T** killed by K1 on H, or inconclusive with too few trades: its
  thresholds were set on perpetual probabilities and may trade at another
  rate on spot.
- Every slot's gross edge below 10 bp of turnover, so K-fee also fires.
- Not predicted: the sign of any gross edge at zero fees, and the size of
  the print rule's bias.

## Fees

**The rule.** Every fill pays the fee of its venue's sourced interval at the
fill's time, in basis points of the fill's notional, charged in the quote
currency. A day is covered when it lies wholly inside one sourced interval of
its venue. **A day that no sourced interval covers is refused**: the read
refuses to open a block that holds one, and a unit test checks now that every
day of the reader-test days, W, D, H and Z is covered. P's days are not
covered yet; a dated fee amendment must cover them before P can open
([amendment protocol](#amendment-protocol)).

**The verdict tier** is the one an account holds without 30-day volume, coin
holdings or programme membership: Binance's **Regular** and Bybit's
**non-VIP**. No discount is applied: the sources do not say whether a Regular
account's BNB discount applied to BTC/FDUSD's taker fee, and Bybit's MNT
discount does not apply to orders sent through the API. Every other tier is a
measurement, labelled with what it requires, and a fee of zero at a tier is
labelled "conditional on <tier> eligibility".

Why these blocks.

- **Every 2026 block starts after BTC/FDUSD's fall** in activity: W in April,
  when the pair is near the level it keeps from May, and D from May. The
  months from January to March hold the pair's 2025-like weeks and the fall
  itself; no block uses them.
- **One calendar for both venues**, so that the print rule's calibration
  compares the same days, and one access, one embargo and one read serve
  the family.
- **The reader-test days** lie in March, outside every block, so the spot
  readers are proven on days no block uses (the spot book exists only from
  2025-04-29, and no earlier spot day is on disk to test on). They are not
  the 2025 study's reader-test days, 2026-01-05 to 07, which no successor may
  put in a block and which this study does not read.
- **D** gives the frozen fits 85 days of training and seven of thresholds,
  and a walk-forward over June and July gives each slot 61 out-of-sample
  days for its σ.
- **E** is a week no slot reads, so that fits frozen on 31 July are first
  scored a week later.
- **H** is every published day after E, to 2026-10-21, the last day the
  archives held when this was registered.
- **Z** is the last quarter of 2025 on the pair the author worked on: the
  closest 2025 days to the 2026 blocks, under the fee interval with the zero
  cell for VIP 2–9. It is inside the author's information set, so it is
  described and never tested, and it is read after H so that nothing on it
  can move anything about H.
- **P** is the first two months after this registration, outside every
  information set. Its embargo, 2026-10-22 to 10-31, holds the registration
  day.

Days are independent: each starts flat, no position is held across midnight,
and the day is the cluster for every statistic. A day is the rows stamped
from its midnight to the next, as in the 2025 registration, §Blocks. Rolling
statistics may read earlier days of the same block, and of W before D. **H's
first day, Z's and P's start cold**, as does a day after a hole (a previous
calendar day without a usable plane on that venue): rolling state is rebuilt
from the block's own rows and the first 61 minutes from midnight are not
scored, 61 minutes being the longest lookback in seconds rounded up (a 60 s
window and 3,600 s of winsorising, as in 2025).

**Exclusions are per day and per venue**, counted by reason and reported per
block. On Bybit's book, the 2025 rules: more sequence gaps than
`max_sequence_gaps` (the 95th percentile of D's per-day gap counts, rounded
up, by the 2025 formula, recorded in the amendment), a `positive_prices`
error in `validate_book`'s report, a price off the day's tick grid, a
missing plane (an HTTP 404 only), an unreadable archive or an unfetchable day
under the 2025 fetch plan's terminal rules, and a day flagged `flatten_stale`
in any run of the block's read. On Binance's prints: a missing file (404
only), a day without a print, a price off the tick grid, an unreadable or
unfetchable file, and a day flagged `flatten_stale` by the print rule
([below](#common-settings)). A day excluded on a venue is excluded from every
series of that venue's slots. **A venue's block is unreadable** when more than
a quarter of its days are excluded on that venue; nothing is retuned and no
status changes on it ([status rules](#status-rules)). Each verdict on Bybit
is also reported with the gap-excluded days put back, as a measurement.

## Common settings

- **Instruments**: Bybit's spot BTCUSDT and Binance's spot BTCFDUSD, each
  keyed by venue and market (`bybit-spot:BTCUSDT`, `binance-spot:BTCFDUSD`),
  never by symbol alone, since Bybit's linear perpetual is also `BTCUSDT`.
  Tick and lot steps come from each venue's spot specification and are
  recorded in the amendment; the off-grid exclusion uses each day's own tick.
- **The book grid** (Bybit) is the 2025 registration's: `data/bybit.reconstruct`
  at `grid_ms=100`, at most one row per 100 ms interval at its first update,
  stamped with that update's venue time, no forward fill.
- **The print grid** (S25-PF, and S25-PB on Bybit's prints): one row per
  100 ms interval that holds at least one print, stamped at its first print
  and carrying the state right after every print with that stamp; later
  prints of the interval belong to the next row; an interval without a print
  has no row. Prints are aligned backward, as in 2025: row `t` holds the
  prints in (s_{t−1}, s_t].
- **Timing**: decisions are made at row stamps; an order is live 10 ms after
  its decision; every quantity in seconds is measured on row stamps, and
  "`t + h`" is the first row stamped at or after `t + h`.
- **Clip**: 0.010 BTC a trade, every slot, no sizing; one position at a time.
  Results are also given per 100 units of quote of clip a day.
- **The day**: no entry decided before 00:01 or after 23:58 UTC; anything open
  at 23:59 is closed by a costed taker order; the accounting identity holds
  at every day's end, with turnover equal to the sum of fill notionals.
  There is no funding.

### The short side

S25 is two-sided, and spot has no short without coins to sell. **Registered:
shorts from held inventory.** Each day starts holding one clip of BTC and
quote worth three clips at the day's first print. A long buys a clip with
quote and sells it at its exit; a short sells the held clip and buys it back
at its exit. A round trip's P&L, fees and turnover are those of a long or a
short of one clip, so every slot keeps S25's trades unchanged.
**The inventory's own P&L**, the clip of BTC marked from the day's first print
to its last, is a separate line, reported and never inside `net_bp_trade`,
any kill or any status. **K-inv** voids a slot whose account would hold less
than zero of either coin at any event; with these balances it can fire only
through an accounting fault.

Measured beside every slot, with no status: the **long-only** variant, which
drops every short decision before the one-position rule (so its trades
differ from the slot's), with K-drift's all-long version as its comparator.
Borrowing to short is not modelled ([choices](#choices-and-their-reasons)).

### Fills

**On a book** (S25-BB, S25-T, and the calibration on Bybit): the 2025
registration's event-time taker executor, unchanged: the clip walks the
first snapshot at or after the decision + 10 ms across ten levels, an entry
whose clip exceeds the visible depth is skipped and counted, an exit's depth
beyond the tenth level is charged at the tenth level's price and counted, the
late-snapshot counters and `flatten_stale` (R16) as there; the fee is the
day's from the table and there is no funding.

**On prints, the side-correct rule** (S25-PF, S25-PB), new code:

- An order arrives at its decision + 10 ms. **A buy fills at the first
  buyer-initiated print stamped at or after its arrival**, a sell at the
  first seller-initiated one: a price at which a taker traded on that side
  at that moment.
- The clip walks that print and the other prints of the same side with the
  same stamp, in their archive order, at their volume-weighted price; any
  part of the clip beyond that sweep is charged at the sweep's last price and
  counted (`fill_beyond_sweep`). No entry is skipped, for depth or for wait.
- The wait from arrival to the fill's print is recorded, and a fill whose
  print comes more than 1 s after arrival is counted as `entry_late_print`
  or `exit_late_print`, a measurement that excludes no day.
- The exit is decided at the first row stamped at or after `t + h`, `t` the
  entry decision, or at 23:59 if that comes first, and fills by the same
  rule, never on a print before the entry's fill. The next decision may be
  taken at a row stamped at or after the exit's fill print.
- An order that no print of its side follows before the day ends is priced at
  the last print of its side before it; if that print is more than 60 s older
  than the order, the day is flagged `flatten_stale` and excluded from every
  series of the venue's block. No print of the next day is read.
- The label's fills and the executor's call the same function.

**The brackets**, each run on the true decisions and reported beside every
print-rule verdict: **optimistic**, the whole clip at the first print's price;
**pessimistic depth**, the part beyond the sweep walking later prints of the
same side; **stale**, the last print of the same side stamped at or before the
decision row; and **worse of two**, per fill the worse of the rule's price and
the stale price, with pessimistic depth. K-fill reads the last one.

## Labels, features and fits

**The print label** (S25-PF, S25-PB, S25-PFT, Z-PF; new code). At row `t` with
hold `h`, for the clip: the entry fills by the print rule at `t` + 10 ms; the
label's exit order is decided at the first row stamped at or after the entry
fill's print + `h` and fills by the same rule. Long P&L = (the exit's sell
price − the entry's buy price) / the mid proxy at the entry fill × 1e4 −
2 × the label fee; short symmetric. Class `long` if the long P&L is positive,
`short` if the short P&L is, and, since prints can make both positive, the
larger of the two when both are, `hold` on a tie or otherwise. A row without
an entry or exit fill before its day ends has no label and is not a
training row. The label fee is 0 for the slot and 0 or 10 bp across its
neighbourhood; the label declares `h`, and the purge is derived from it.

**The walked-book label** (S25-BB): the 2025 registration's, unchanged, on
the spot book, at 0 bp for the slot and 0 or 10 bp across its neighbourhood.

**The nine print members** of S25's list, rebuilt on the print grid:
`flow_imbalance_6`, `flow_imbalance_24`, `flow_imbalance_120`,
`trade_intensity_6`, `trade_intensity_24`, `trade_intensity_120`,
`flow_persistence_24`, `vwap_deviation_bp` and `trade_run_length_60s`.

- Their row windows (6, 24 and 120 rows) count rows of the print grid, so a
  window spans a varying time; this is a registered change of meaning, not a
  value chosen on data.
- `vwap_deviation_bp` divides a row's volume-weighted print price by the
  previous row's mid proxy, in place of the book's mid.
- `trade_run_length_60s` is evaluated at print-grid stamps.
- Each is winsorised at the 0.1% and 99.9% quantiles of its own trailing
  3,600 s, as in 2025. None is in raw size units, so none is scaled.
- Every one passes `validation/leakage.py` before any D run, with the prints
  cut at a time strictly inside a row interval (K-leak). They are built in a
  registry of their own, never in the global one.

**S25-BB's inputs** are the 40 features as the 2025 study computes them, on
the spot book, with `ofi_1` and `ofi_20` divided by the median touch size of
Bybit's spot book on W, written to this study's fitted-values file. **S25-T's**
are the same 40 divided by the 2025 study's own touch size, from its
fitted-values file, so that its fit sees its inputs on the scale it was
trained on.

**Fits.** `gbm` (`models/gbm.py`), three classes, the repository's defaults,
seed 42, fitted on every tenth row of the training days, at most 4 million
rows taken evenly in time, as in 2025. θ per side by the 2025 hold-first rule
at the cell's rate, on the inner validation's every row. A purge of the hold
plus 61 minutes precedes each inner validation and each test month. On D, for
S25-PF and S25-BB:

| Fit | Training days | θ on | Scored on |
|---|---|---|---|
| June fold | 2026-05-01 to 05-24 | 05-25 to 05-31 | June |
| July fold | 2026-05-01 to 06-23 | 06-24 to 06-30 | July |
| Frozen | 2026-05-01 to 07-24 | 07-25 to 07-31 | H (and P) |

Each fit is made for every hold and label fee of the slot's neighbourhood (6
per fold, 18 per slot), so K-nbhd on H runs only frozen files. S25-PB and
S25-PFT are fitted for their own cell only (3 fits each). S25-T is never
fitted. **The fold-level leakage test** of the 2025 registration runs on
every fold of S25-PF and S25-BB: mutating every price after the fold's cutoff
leaves its weights and θ unchanged, by sha256.

### Nothing is searched

No value of this registration is chosen on an outcome: the slots, their

and the days it read; since it can choose nothing, its only use would be to
motivate an amendment, which the [protocol](#amendment-protocol) forbids.

## Metrics

- **`net_bp_trade`**, primary for every slot: for one round trip, its P&L in
  quote (a long's exit value less its entry value, a short's entry value less
  its exit value) over the entry notional, × 1e4, less both sides' fees in bp
  of the entry notional. A day's value is the day's net in quote summed over
  its round trips, over the sum of their entry notionals, × 1e4. The daily
  series has one value per day with at least one trade.
- **`gross_bp_trade`**, the same before fees: the result at zero fees,
  reported for every slot, labelled "conditional on a zero-fee venue".
- **`gross_bp_turnover`**: gross over turnover (both sides' notional), × 1e4,
  the fee break-even per side, reported against every tier of the venue.
- **IC**: per day, the rank correlation of `p_long − p_short` with the
  realised forward move over `h`, over all decision rows; on a book, mid to
  mid as in 2025; on prints, from the mid proxy at the entry fill to the mid
  proxy at the first row stamped at or after the fill + `h`. Its mean over
  days.
- **The daily series decides.** The mean, the day-level t, the Newey–West t
  (Bartlett, lag 5 positions), the median day, K1, K-days, the placebos,
  K-nbhd, σ_D and the minimum detectable effect are computed on the daily
  series, never on trade rows, through `evaluation/significance.assess` with
  one row per day; a series of fewer than two days fails the day count and
  enters Holm with p = 1. The one-sided p-value is
  `market_making/verdicts.day_t(series).p_one_sided`, Holm is
  `verdicts.holm`.
- **Minimum detectable effect**, recorded in the amendment per slot:
  `(3 + 0.84) × σ_D / √n_eff`, σ_D the standard deviation of the slot's daily
  series over D's 61 test days and `n_eff` = 75 × the share of those days
  with a trade; on P, P's usable days in place of 75, with σ_D and the share
  as frozen.
- **Beside every verdict**: trades a day, the share of days positive, the
  median day, the maximum drawdown, net in quote a day, the result at every
  tier, by side, the long-only variant, the inventory's own P&L, the slot's
  30-day traded volume, the counters (skipped entries, late snapshots or
  prints, `fill_beyond_sweep`, depth beyond the tenth level), and for a print
  slot every bracket.

## Status rules

**The family** is **{S25-PF, S25-BB, S25-T}, with m = 3** on H and on P.

**The bar.** A slot passes on a block when no kill condition fires and all
of these hold on its daily series: its one-sided day-level p-value passes
Holm's procedure at 5% with **m = 3**; the day-level t is at least 3; the
Newey–West t is at least 2; the median day is positive; and the series has
at least 20 days with a trade on H, 10 on P. At m = 3 Holm's first step
needs a t of 2.17 at 74 degrees of freedom, 2.29 at 19 and 2.51 at 9, so
the t ≥ 3 bar binds on every block. A slot enters Holm with p = 1 when it is void, killed,
inconclusive with too few trades, not assessed, or not taken to the block.

**Precedence**, the same on every block, one status per result:

1. **void** — K-leak, K-units, K-acct, K-cap or K-inv fires; counted as
   killed.
2. **inconclusive (too few trades)** — K-trades: fewer than 100 round trips
   over the block. Nothing below is evaluated.
3. **killed** — any kill condition fires.
4. **candidate** (on H) or **passes** (on P) — the bar is met.
5. **inconclusive** — otherwise; labelled "below the minimum detectable
   effect" when the mean is smaller in size than the MDE.

**P.** Only a candidate on H is taken to P. P is read once, in one session
for every candidate, after the last day of P is published on both venues and
a dated fee amendment covers P's days; every other slot and measured row is
computed in that session as a measurement.

| On H | On P | Status |
|---|---|---|
| candidate | passes | **confirmed** |
| candidate | does not pass, no kill | **candidate, not confirmed** |
| candidate | a kill fires | **killed on P** |
| candidate | a void condition fires | **void** (counted as killed) |
| candidate | P unreadable, or its read failed | **candidate, not confirmed (P unreadable)** |
| candidate | not read | **candidate (P not read)** |
| any other status | not read | that status |

**Unreadable and failed reads.** A venue's block with more than a quarter of
its days excluded is unreadable: on H, that venue's slots are `not assessed
(H unreadable on <venue>)`, the other venue's are assessed, and H is spent.
A read that fails is resumed at the same commit; if the same stage fails
again with the same exception, or the read has not finished after five
resumptions, the session closes with every slot of the block `not assessed
(<block> read failed)`, as in the 2025 registration, §Access.

**No number** at a tier other than the verdict tier, at zero fees, under a
bracket or the long-only variant, with the gap-excluded days put back, of a
measured row, on Z, or in the inventory's P&L **may be described as the
strategy working.** Such numbers are measurements. In particular, no account
could trade either pair at zero taker fees on any day of W, D, H or P as far
as the sources show, so a positive gross edge is a statement about a venue
that did not exist for these pairs in 2026.

### Kill conditions per slot

| Slot | Execution | Primary series | Kills | Reported beside |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S25-PF | taker, side-correct print rule | `net_bp_trade` at Regular | K1, K-label, K-placebo, K-drift, K-nbhd, K-days, K-fee, K-latency, **K-fill** | every bracket; the calibration on Bybit (S25-PB); the waits and late prints |
| S25-BB | taker, book walk | `net_bp_trade` at non-VIP | K1, K-label, K-placebo, K-drift, K-nbhd, K-days, K-fee, K-latency | the gap-excluded days put back |
| S25-T | taker, book walk, the 2025 frozen fit | `net_bp_trade` at non-VIP | as S25-BB | its trade rate against 240 a day |

Beside every slot: the long-only variant, the inventory's own P&L, the
result at every tier and at zero fees, and its 30-day traded volume.

Void conditions; the slot is recorded as `void`:

- **K-leak** — a feature fails `validation/leakage.py`, or the fold-level
  leakage test fails; every result of the study is void.
- **K-units** — a cost (a fee, the walk of a book, a print fill) or the
  hold-first rule's input reaches the result through a normalised column
  rather than prices.
- **K-acct** — the accounting identity fails on any day, or turnover differs
  from the sum of fill notionals, in any run of the slot's read.
- **K-cap** — the traded position exceeds one clip either way at any event.
- **K-inv** — the account would hold less than zero of either coin at any
  event ([the short side](#the-short-side)).

Kill conditions:

- **K1** — the mean of the slot's daily series on the block is ≤ 0.
- **K-label** — the signal's IC on the block is ≤ 0.
- **K-placebo** — the true mean does not exceed each of: the 95th percentile
  of the 200 random-direction draws; the sign-flipped signal's mean; and the
  one-day-shifted signal's mean.
- **K-drift** — the true mean does not exceed the larger of the all-long and
  the all-short versions' means at the same entry times.
- **K-nbhd** — the median, over the slot's 12 neighbourhood cells, of each
  cell's mean on the block is ≤ 0; a cell without a trade scores 0. Only the
  frozen fits and θ run.
- **K-days** — half or fewer of the series' days are positive.
- **K-fee** — the fee break-even per side, `gross_bp_turnover`, is at or
  below the verdict tier's taker fee, 10 bp. For a positive net it can only
  fire through an accounting fault; its main use is the report of how far
  each slot was from paying.
- **K-latency** — a positive result that is ≤ 0 when every order is live
  110 ms after its decision (on a book, one more 100 ms row; on prints, the
  arrival moved by 100 ms).
- **K-fill** (print slots) — a positive result that is not positive under
  the worse-of-two bracket ([fills](#fills)).

Not kills, but they set the status: **K-trades**, fewer than 100 round trips
over the block, makes the result inconclusive whatever its sign, before any
kill; **K-MDE**, a result that does not pass, with no kill, and is smaller
in size than the MDE, is "inconclusive, below the minimum detectable effect".
K-latency and K-fill fire only when the result is positive under the default
rules.

### Placebos

Each is run through the same execution, fee, account and day rules as the
true slot, on the same block and the true slot's entry decision times; a
taker's exit time does not depend on its side, so no decision is skipped.

| Placebo | What changes | Seeds |
|---|---|---|
| sign-flipped | every direction reversed | — |
| random direction | each direction drawn at random | 200 draws, seeds 0–199 |
| one-day shift | each usable day takes the previous usable day's signal tape at the same time of day, circularly over the block's usable days, stepping over excluded days as in 2025 | — |
| all-long, all-short | one direction at every entry time (K-drift) | — |

The book slots' placebos, neighbourhood cells and K-latency come from walk
tables built from the 2025 executor's walk; the print slots' from walk tables
built from the print rule's fill function, proven equal to the print
executor at stage 0.

## Amendment protocol

**The D amendment**, after D and before H is read, is one dated entry under
[Amendments](#amendments), written by the entry point and reviewed before it
is committed. It records: the days excluded on D, per venue and reason; the
sequence-gap threshold by its formula; each instrument's tick and lot steps;
the reader-test results (each day's message counts, median interval,
sequence gaps and findings, and the print parsers' checks); the sha256 of
every frozen fit, of each slot's frozen file and of the fitted-values file;
every θ of every neighbourhood cell; σ_D and the minimum detectable effect of
each slot; the D series' summaries against the two nulls, labelled
"development"; the calibration on D; the compute record; and the
configuration's sha256 after the frozen values are written into its
placeholders, on a line of the form ``Frozen configuration sha256:
`<hash>` ``. Each value has one placeholder in the configuration, commented
"frozen by the D amendment". Nothing else may change.

**A correction before H** (a bug in a reader, a feature, the label, the
print rule, the executor or the accounting) is made only by a further dated
amendment that states it and, if it could change a D number, re-runs D in
full before anything is frozen; complete D runs are numbered. Every change to
the study code after the code-freeze note is such a correction. No amendment
may cite a D number, a result of the 2025 study or anything seen on spot data
as its reason, except a fault it names.

**The fee amendment** is the only amendment after H: it transcribes, from
sources of the kinds listed under [Fees](#fees), the schedules in force on P's
days, before P is opened, and adds intervals only.

**After H is read**, nothing is changed and nothing is re-run under another
rule. **A defect found after a read can only lower a status**: it is stated
in `docs/spot_2026_defects.md`, naming the mechanism by which it could have
raised a result; every status it could affect becomes `void (defect found
after the <block> read)`, which counts as killed and enters Holm with p = 1
on later blocks. Nothing can raise a status; a corrected run is a successor
registration.

**The ledger rule.** Only reads through the ledger count. A number computed
on H, Z or P outside it is not a result and cannot change a status.

## What this study cannot establish

- **The ask and the bid at a Binance order's arrival.** There is no book;
  the print rule is a proxy, bracketed, and calibrated on another venue.
- **What S25 earned on BTC/FDUSD in 2025.** Those days are inside the
  author's information set; Z describes them and tests nothing.
- **The pair as it traded before 2026-01-29.** 2026's BTC/FDUSD is a thinner
  market; a result on H is about the pair after the change.
- **The fee a given account paid.** Tiers depend on volume and holdings; the
  verdict is at the tier that needs neither, and whether a BNB discount
  applied to a Regular account's taker fee on the pair is not known.
- **Capacity.** The clip is 0.010 BTC and own impact is not modelled; on a
  thin BTC/FDUSD day a clip can be a large share of a minute's prints, which
  `fill_beyond_sweep` and the activity beside each series show.
- **Shorting through margin.** Shorts come from held inventory; borrow costs,
  borrow limits and liquidation are not modelled.
- **Quote-currency risk.** Results are in FDUSD or USDT and never converted;
  a stablecoin leaving its peg moves the pair's prices and is in the result.
- **Passive entry on spot.** Without a book on Binance no queue can be
  simulated; the 2025 study measures passive entry on the perpetual.
- **Anything beyond two pairs, two venues and the 2026 blocks.**

## Choices and their reasons

- **The primary venue is the pair the question names**, Binance BTC/FDUSD,
  though it has no book; Bybit's spot book is the secondary venue, where S25
  runs as registered and where the print rule can be checked against a book.
- **Every block in 2026 after the pair's fall in activity**: 2025 is inside
  the author's information set, and January to March 2026 hold the end of
  the 2025 market and the fall itself, so a block there would mix two
  markets. One calendar serves both venues.
- **Z is described, not developed on.** The pair changed on 2026-01-29; a fit
  on 2025 would test transfer across that change and carry the information
  set into the fit. Z is read after H, so it can move nothing.
- **A prospective P.** H alone can make a slot no more than a candidate; P's
  days lie after this registration, outside every information set, and cost
  nothing unless a slot passes on H.
- **Shorts from held inventory, not long-only.** Inventory keeps S25's
  trades unchanged, so the test is of S25 and not of a one-sided strategy;
  long-only is measured beside it. Borrowing was not chosen: it needs a margin
  account and carries liquidation, while its cost, about 0.35% to 1.3% a year
  on Binance in the window (B20), is under 0.02 bp an hour, which held
  inventory matches without either.
- **The nine print members as the slot, the twelve touch proxies measured.**
  The nine are S25's own members; the proxies add construction choices made
  of prints. The 19 size and depth members have no analogue.
- **The side-correct print rule.** It prices a fill at a price a taker paid
  on that side at that moment. The last print before the decision is stale
  and rewards predicting the past; one-second bars with an assumed spread
  would make the result mostly the assumed spread. No entry is skipped for
  its wait, because a skip conditioned on a print following soon selects
  decisions by what happens after them. K-fill holds a verdict to the
  bracket least favourable to it.
- **The verdict tier is the one an account holds with no volume or
  holdings.** A slot trading 240 clips a day has volume of its own, which is
  reported; but tiers also need coin holdings, and 2026's thresholds were not
  transcribed, so no other tier is assumed.
- **S25-T beside S25-BB.** S25-T has no degree of freedom left; S25-BB asks
  whether the recipe works when allowed to learn spot's scales. S25-T's
  inputs keep its own scale, so that it sees what it was trained on.
- **No search, m = 3, neighbourhoods of 12.** This is a confirmation of S25,
  so nothing is chosen on an outcome and no placebo search is needed. The
  neighbourhood keeps S25's hold, rate and label-fee axes; its label-fee step
  is each venue's verdict taker fee, as 5.5 bp was on the perpetual; the
  regime axis is dropped, since S25 has no regime policy and the detector's
  flags are not computed on spot.
- **Pairs not taken.** Binance BTC/U charged nothing to anyone from
  2026-04-17 to 07-16 (B17), but it was listed on 2026-01-13, it is thin, and
  its window would have to hold both a development and a held-out block in 91
  days. Binance BTC/USDT and BTC/USDC have no reduced taker fee for a Regular
  account beyond USDC's 9.5 bp. Bybit's BTCUSDC discount to 5 bp applies only
  to VIP accounts trading by hand, with API volume under 20%, which a
  strategy sending orders through the API cannot meet.
- **The reader-test days in March**, outside every block and apart from the
  2025 study's.
- **Nothing is fetched before the 2025 study's last read**: the two studies'
  disk peaks do not fit together on the machine, and the 2025 study's data
  roots, environment and code stay untouched until its reads end.
