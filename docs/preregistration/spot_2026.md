## Why this study

The pipeline trades perpetual futures only: Bybit's linear books and prints,
and Binance's futures bars. This study adds spot, where there is no funding,
no short without borrowed or held coins, a different fee schedule on every
pair, and, on Binance, no public order book.

It asks one question, in three forms fixed in advance: **does S25 pay on spot
at the fees charged on each day of a block nobody has seen?** S25 is boosted
trees on a book's microstructure, a hold-first rule at 240 trades a day, a
50 s hold closed by time, taker in and taker out, one clip of 0.010 BTC.

- **The primary venue is Binance BTC/FDUSD.** The author worked on this pair in
  2025, so every 2025 day of it is inside the author's information set and
  none can be a blind test: the blind blocks are in 2026. Binance's public
  archives hold the pair's prints and bars and no book, so S25 itself cannot
  be computed there (31 of its 40 inputs read the book, and its label and its
  executor walk it). The primary slot is S25's **prints-only variant**,
  S25-PF: S25's coordinates with its nine print inputs, on a grid of prints,
  filled by a side-correct print rule whose bias is bracketed and measured.
- **The secondary venue is Bybit spot BTCUSDT**, which has a 200-level book
  from 2025-04-29 and prints from 2022. There S25 runs as registered: refitted
  on spot books (S25-BB), and unchanged, the 2025 study's frozen fit applied
  to spot books with nothing refitted (S25-T).

Every verdict is taken at the fee the venue charged an account without volume
or holdings on that day, from a sourced, dated table; a day no sourced
interval covers is refused. Cheaper tiers, and fees of zero, are measurements.

**The prior is negative** ([below](#expected-outcome-stated-in-advance)). The
study is built so that a negative answer says how far each slot was from
paying: every verdict carries its fee break-even.

## What was known before this was written

Everything below was available when the design was chosen, and some of it
shaped the design. None of it is a spot number computed by this repository.

1. **General market knowledge.** BTC's daily price path to 2026-09-30 is in
   this repository's README, in the break figure drawn from Binance's BTCUSDT
   daily futures bars from 2024-01-01 to 2026-09-30, which are on disk. The
   paragraph under it names two scale breaks of the shorter-memory setting,
   **2026-02-06 up** and **2026-08-14 down**. The second lies inside this
   study's held-out block H. Neither set a boundary here.
2. **The author worked on BTC/FDUSD in 2025.** This registration says nothing
   more about it; it is a fact about the information set.
3. **This repository's 2025 study**: its registration, configuration and
   code, S25's coordinates, the sha256 of every frozen fit and of S25's frozen
   slot file, and its D amendment of 2026-10-18, which holds development
   numbers on Bybit's BTCUSDT perpetual for January to June 2025. None of them
   sets a value here, and none is quoted. Its H, F and P will be read before
   this study's development block (the [data budget](#data-budget) explains
   why); their results, on 2025 perpetual data, will be known when this
   study's D and H are read, and can change nothing here. Its three
   reader-test days, 2026-01-05 to 07, of Bybit's linear book, were read for
   message counts; they lie in no block of this study.
4. **This repository's earlier studies on perpetuals** were killed
   ([`docs/findings.md`](../findings.md)); they are the reason for the prior.
5. **Fee schedules**, sourced on 2026-10-22 and 23 from the venues'
   announcements, fee pages, public endpoints and archived captures of them:
   the table under [Fees](#fees). In particular, Binance BTC/FDUSD charged
   0 maker and 10 bp taker to Regular and VIP 1 accounts throughout 2025 and
   2026, and nothing to VIP 2–9 and liquidity-provider accounts until
   2026-01-29; Bybit spot BTCUSDT charged 10 bp on both sides to non-VIP
   accounts throughout. Also known: Binance's BTC/U pair charged nothing to
   anyone from 2026-04-17 to 07-16; BTC margin borrow rates on both venues;
   and FDUSD's departure from its peg on 2025-04-02 (public news).
6. **Archive metadata, read for this registration on 2026-10-22**, by HTTP
   `HEAD` requests and directory listings only: every day of Bybit's spot
   `ob200` book archive from 2025-04-01 to 2026-10-22, every daily file of its
   spot print listing, the S3 listings (key, size, time) of Binance's spot
   archives for BTCFDUSD and, for reference, BTCUSDT and BTCUSDC, 71 `HEAD`
   requests that matched those listings, and the absence of any spot book
   data on `data.binance.vision`. A file's size is a rough proxy for a day's
   activity, and the sizes showed **BTC/FDUSD's activity falling about
   30-fold after 2026-01-28**, to near 1 MB of trades a day from May 2026
   ([Data](#data)). **The block boundaries were set from that fall**: every
   2026 block starts after it. No other value was set from the sizes, except
   the fetch sizes and the disk budget.
7. **Nothing else.** No spot book, print, trade or bar of any year has been
   fetched or opened by this repository; the raw Binance files on disk are
   futures files, and the daily bars of item 1 are futures bars.

**The information set on BTC/FDUSD** is taken to end on 2025-12-31. The study
does not rest on that date alone: its first scored day is 2026-05-01, and its
held-out block starts on 2026-08-08.
